# Business Rules

BR-001 Every entity has a UUID.

BR-002 Soft delete by default.

BR-003 A floor can have multiple campaigns.

BR-004 Only one published model per floor.

BR-005 Datasets are immutable.

BR-006 Model versions are immutable.

BR-007 Fingerprints cannot exist without a campaign.

BR-008 Cells cannot overlap.

BR-009 Every fingerprint belongs to exactly one cell.

BR-010 Floor plans are versioned.
